# Official Law Seeds

This directory is for manually reviewed official law seed files used when MOLEG live transport is unavailable or unstable.

Rules:

- Do not store raw MOLEG JSON/XML payloads.
- Do not store full article body text.
- Do not include API keys, OC values, serviceKey values, tokens, or full secret-bearing URLs.
- Store only sanitized summaries, article titles, anchors/references, procedure codes, and source metadata.
- Do not guess article numbers, thresholds, or review requirements. Fill them only after official source review.

Files:

- `urban_development_act.seed.yaml`
- `urban_development_act_enforcement_decree.seed.yaml`
- `urban_development_act_enforcement_rule.seed.yaml`
- `examples/official_law_seed.example.yaml`

The three urban-development seed files are intentionally empty templates until reviewed official content is entered.
